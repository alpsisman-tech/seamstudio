Cookie & Tracking Notice

The short version: our public sites don't track you for advertising or analytics. Here's exactly what they do use.

Last updated: 8 July 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website can store on your device. Similar technologies (local storage, pixels, web fonts) can also read or receive limited information from your browser. Under UK PECR and the EU ePrivacy rules, non-essential cookies require your consent; strictly necessary ones do not.

2. What we use

Seam Studio's marketing websites are deliberately lightweight. We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or analytics products such as Google Analytics. What may be present:

TechnologyTypePurposeConsent needed?
Essential local storageFirst-partyRemembering basic UI state (e.g. a dismissed banner) if used on a pageNo — strictly necessary
Google FontsThird-partyServing the site's typefaces; Google receives your IP address when a font loadsNot a cookie, but a data transfer — see below
Netlify / FormSubmitThird-partyHosting and secure delivery of form submissions; may set a security/anti-abuse tokenNo — strictly necessary

3. A note on Google Fonts

We load fonts from Google's servers, which means Google receives your IP address to deliver them. If you'd prefer to avoid this entirely, we can self-host the fonts — and we may move to self-hosting to minimise data sharing. This is disclosed here for transparency.

4. Managing cookies

Because we don't set non-essential cookies, there's nothing to opt out of for tracking. You can still control or clear cookies and site data at any time through your browser settings, and block third-party content — though blocking may affect how fonts render.

5. Changes

If we ever add analytics or other non-essential technologies, we'll update this notice and, where required, ask for your consent through a banner before they run.

6. More information

See our Privacy Policy for how we handle personal data, and our KVKK Aydınlatma Metni for Türkiye. Questions: [privacy@seamuk.com] or the contact form.
