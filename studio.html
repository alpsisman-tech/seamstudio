The studio behind the ventures

A studio that starts
on the floor, not the slide.

Seam Studio is small on purpose. We build a few AI products deeply, each rooted in a real business, rather than many shallow ones. This is what we believe and how we got here.

The story

It began in a textile factory in İstanbul.

Seam Studio didn't start with a pitch deck. It started on the floor of a working made-to-order textile manufacturer — watching quality inspectors squint at fabric under bad light, and watching the sales desk drown in request-for-quotation emails that all needed the same painstaking pricing math.

Two problems, one root cause: skilled people spending their day on work a machine could assist with, while the judgement that actually mattered got squeezed. So we built. Seam taught a vision model to see fabric defects. Quotewright learned the factory's own catalog and started drafting quotes. The tools worked because they were forged where the work happens.

From there the pattern generalised. Misafir took the same draft-first, human-gated philosophy to hospitality — reading guest messages, drafting replies, keeping the owner in control. Three products, one method. That method is the studio.

What we believe

Six principles we don't compromise on.

They sound simple. Holding to all six, in every product, under real deadlines — that's the hard part, and the whole point.

01

Build on real operations

If it hasn't survived a real mill, inbox, or front desk, it isn't done. We prototype on live data, not curated demos.

02

Keep a human in the loop

AI drafts and flags; people decide and send. Accountability never leaves the person who owns the outcome.

03

Ground every output

Prices from your catalog, detections with evidence, replies without invention. Unsure means asking, not guessing.

04

Design for the loop

Every correction improves the model. We build products that compound in value the more they're used.

05

Meet people in their language

Multilingual is a starting assumption. The businesses we serve trade across borders; so do our tools.

06

Ship small, then widen

Pilots first — quick to set up, easy to leave. We earn scope by proving value on a floor you already run.

The path so far

From one floor to a studio.

On the floor

The first problem

Watching inspectors and the sales desk in a real İstanbul textile factory. The recurring theme: skilled judgement buried under repetitive, error-prone work.

Venture 01

Seam is born

A vision model trained to detect fabric defects — holes, stains, streaks and more — with bounding boxes, confidence, and a dashboard a QC team would actually use.

Venture 02

Quotewright follows

The same factory's RFQ inbox becomes the training ground for a tool that drafts complete, catalog-grounded quotes — cash and term — awaiting one-tap approval.

Venture 03

Misafir generalises the method

Draft-first, human-gated AI moves into hospitality: reading guest messages, drafting multilingual replies, and briefing owners weekly.

Today

Seam Studio

Three products under one roof and one method — with room for the next problem we meet on a floor that deserves better tools.
