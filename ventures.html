01 — Seam
Seam
Textile quality control

A vision-AI platform where manufacturers upload fabric images and get instant, evidence-backed defect detection — then watch it roll up into a live dashboard. Seam locates holes, stains, streaks, weft & warp defects, needle marks, broken stitches, pinched fabric and knot/slubs, with confidence scores and an analyst that explains the causes.

Misafir
Hospitality guest care

Misafir watches reviews and guest messages across channels, reads language, intent and sentiment, then drafts a reply in the guest's own language — and waits behind a human approval gate. It also compiles a weekly owner's brief, so a hotelier wakes up to what happened and what needs a decision.
