Venture 01
Seam
AI textile defect detection

A vision-AI platform where manufacturers upload fabric images and get instant, evidence-backed defect detection. Seam spots holes, stains, streaks, weft & warp defects, needle marks, broken stitches, pinched fabric and knot/slubs — with bounding boxes, confidence scores, and a Claude-powered analyst that explains the why behind the numbers.

Venture 03
Misafir
An AI maître for hospitality

Misafir watches reviews and guest messages across channels, detects language, intent and sentiment, then drafts a reply in the guest's own language — and waits behind a human approval gate. It also compiles a weekly owner's brief, so a hotelier wakes up to what happened and what needs a decision.

The shared DNA

Different industries. Same spine.

Three products that look nothing alike are built on the same four convictions — that's what makes them a studio, not a portfolio.

Draft-first, human-gated

Seam flags, Quotewright quotes, Misafir replies — but a person always approves before anything reaches a customer. The AI removes the drudgery, never the accountability.

Grounded, never fabricated

Prices from your catalog. Detections with evidence. Replies that don't invent facts. When the system is unsure, it says what it needs instead of guessing.

Improves with every use

Each correction and approval is a training signal. The products are designed to compound — sharper detection, better drafts, tighter fit to your business over time.

Multilingual from day one

Turkish, English, German, Italian. Our tools speak the languages the businesses we serve already trade in — localisation is an assumption, not an add-on.
