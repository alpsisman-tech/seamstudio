The Work

Three products, one method.

Each is a full product — designed for a real industry, kept under human control, and grounded in its users' own data. Here is each in depth.

01 — Seam
Seam
Textile quality control

A vision-AI platform where manufacturers upload fabric images and get instant, evidence-backed defect detection. Seam locates holes, stains, streaks, weft & warp defects, needle marks, broken stitches, pinched fabric and knot/slubs — with bounding boxes, confidence scores, and an analyst that explains the causes.

02 — Quotewright
Quotewright
Manufacturing quotes

Quotewright reads every request-for-quotation email a manufacturer receives, matches it to their own price catalog, and drafts a complete quote — cash and term prices, box math done, in the customer's language. You approve with one tap; nothing sends without you, and no price is ever invented.

Reads the whole thread, not the last emailFollow-ups merged

03 — Misafir
Misafir
Hospitality guest care

Misafir watches reviews and guest messages across channels, reads language, intent and sentiment, then drafts a reply in the guest's own language — and waits behind a human approval gate. It also compiles a weekly owner's brief, so a hotelier wakes up to what happened and what needs a decision.
