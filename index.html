How we work

A studio playbook, not a science project.

The same convictions run through every product we build. They're the reason our tools survive contact with a real business.

Forged on real operations

Nothing is trained on tidy demos. Seam learned on a real mill's fabric; Quotewright grew inside a working textile factory's inbox; Misafir was shaped around how hotels actually answer guests. The hard parts are already survived.

Field-firstReal data

A human always holds the pen

AI drafts; people decide. Every quote, every reply, every correction passes a human gate before it reaches a customer. It's not a limitation — it's how trust is built into the workflow.

Approve & sendNo auto-send

Never invent what we can't back

Prices come only from your catalog. Detections come with evidence and confidence. Replies never fabricate a fact. If the system doesn't know, it says what it needs — it doesn't guess.

AuditableEvidence-led

It gets sharper with use

Every QC review, every approved edit, every correction feeds back in. Our products are designed to improve as your team works — a compounding loop, not a static model that decays.

Feedback loopsContinuous

Multilingual, multi-market

Turkish, English, German, Italian — our tools meet customers in their own language because the businesses we build for already do. Localisation isn't a feature bolted on; it's a starting assumption.

TR / EN / DE / ITGlobal-ready

Ship small, ship real

We start with a pilot on a mailbox, a line, a venue you choose — days to set up, easy to pull. Value shows up before a big commitment does. Then we widen from what's already working.

Pilot-firstLow risk
From floor to feature

How an idea becomes a product here.

01

Listen on site

We sit with the mill, the factory inbox, the front desk. The problem defines itself before a line of code is written.

02

Prototype fast

A rough, honest working version — on real data, not staged samples. If it doesn't hold up in the field, we learn that early.

03

Deploy on a real floor

A live pilot on a mailbox, line, or venue you pick. Human-gated from day one, so nothing risky goes out.

04

Iterate with the loop

Every correction and approval feeds back. Accuracy climbs, scope widens, and the product settles into the daily routine.
