1. Who we are

Seam Studio is an AI product studio operating the websites at seamuk.com and its subdomains (including seamstudio, seam, quotewright and misafir). Depending on where you are located, the “data controller” responsible for your personal data is:

  • For visitors in the UK, EU/EEA and elsewhere: Seam Studio Ltd, a company registered in England & Wales, company number [UK company number], registered office [UK registered address], London, United Kingdom.
  • For visitors in Türkiye (Turkey): Seam Studio Teknoloji A.Ş., MERSİS no. [MERSİS number], [registered address], İstanbul, Türkiye — acting as “veri sorumlusu” under the KVKK.

Where both entities are involved, they act as joint controllers and you may exercise your rights against either. Throughout this policy, “we”, “us” and “Seam Studio” refer to the relevant controller above.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy covers personal data we collect through our public websites — principally when you submit an enquiry or pilot-request form, and the limited technical data our hosting provider processes when you visit. Our software products (for example the Seam dashboard) are covered by their own product privacy terms and, where we handle data on behalf of a business customer, by a separate data processing agreement.

3. What data we collect

Information you give us

When you use a contact or pilot-request form, we collect the details you enter, which may include: your name, email address, company/organisation, your area of interest, and the content of your message. You choose what to put in the free-text field, so please don't include sensitive information you'd rather not share.

Information collected automatically

Our sites are static and we do not run advertising or analytics trackers. However, our hosting and form providers automatically process limited technical data needed to deliver the site and prevent abuse — such as your IP address, browser/device type, and timestamps — in server logs. Our web fonts are served by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when fonts load (see Cookies & tracking).

6. International transfers

Some providers above are located outside the UK/EEA (notably the USA). Where we transfer personal data internationally, we rely on an appropriate safeguard — such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement/Addendum, the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or an adequacy decision (including the UK/EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider participates). You can ask us for details of the safeguard used.

7. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry data only as long as needed to deal with your request and for a reasonable period afterwards — typically up to 24 months from our last contact — unless a longer period is required by law or to establish or defend legal claims. Server logs are kept for a short period by our hosting provider. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

8. Your rights

Subject to conditions in the law, you have the right to: access your data; have it corrected; have it erased; restrict or object to our processing; data portability; and to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting prior processing). To exercise any of these, contact us (section 14). We will respond within the statutory time limit (one month under UK/EU GDPR).

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to a regulator — in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), ico.org.uk; in the EU, your local supervisory authority; in Türkiye, the KVKK Kurumu (see below). We'd appreciate the chance to resolve it first.

9. Turkey — KVKK

If you are in Türkiye, our processing is also governed by Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK). Your KVKK rights under Article 11 and how we meet our disclosure (aydınlatma) obligation are set out in our dedicated notice: KVKK Aydınlatma Metni.

10. Cookies & similar technologies

Our public sites do not use advertising or analytics cookies. For the full detail — including the essential storage some pages use and the third-party fonts we load — see our Cookie & Tracking Notice.

11. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls, and vetting the providers we work with. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and to notify you and the regulator of any breach where the law requires.

12. Children

Our sites and products are intended for businesses and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top shows when. Material changes will be highlighted on this page.

14. Contact us

For any privacy question or to exercise your rights, email [privacy@seamuk.com] or use our contact form. You can also write to us at the registered address of the relevant controller in section 1.
